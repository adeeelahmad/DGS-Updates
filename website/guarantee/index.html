How our guarantee works

We make two promises to every Revenue Engine and Scale client. This page explains exactly what they are, who qualifies, how we measure results, and what happens if we miss. No fine print beyond what's written here and in your service agreement.

In plain English

Your phones get answered 24/7 within 14 days, or you don't pay your first monthly fee until they are. And if your revenue hasn't doubled within 90 days of your system going live, and you've done your part, we keep working with no monthly fee for up to 90 more days until it has.

Our two promises

Promise 1

The Live-in-14 Promise

Within 14 days of your kickoff call, every call to your business is answered 24/7 by your AI receptionist, and every missed call gets a text back within 60 seconds.

If we miss it: we don't charge your first monthly fee until it's live.

Promise 2

The Double-or-Work-Free Guarantee

Within 90 days of your system going live, your revenue reaches at least twice your starting baseline.

If we miss it: we keep working on your account with no monthly fee for up to 90 more days, until it does.

Who the Double-or-Work-Free Guarantee applies to

We only offer it when our audit shows the business can realistically double. Before you sign, we confirm in writing that your business qualifies. To qualify you need:

  • A Revenue Engine or Scale plan
  • At least 3 months of revenue history you can verify (bank statements, point-of-sale or accounting reports)
  • Existing demand: a website, a Google Business Profile and regular inbound calls or enquiries
  • The capacity to handle twice as many customers (staff, schedule, equipment)
  • A typical sales cycle under about 30 days from first contact to payment

Adjustments. To keep the comparison fair, we leave out: revenue from price increases made during the period, one-off contracts more than 5× your average job, revenue from a new location or a business you acquire, and normal seasonal swings (compared against the same months last year when those records exist).

Your part

The guarantee depends on these actions, because they're what our most successful clients do. If any of them doesn't happen, the Double-or-Work-Free Guarantee no longer applies, but your service continues as normal.

  1. Complete the onboarding form and send the requested access and assets within 5 days of signing.
  2. Keep your ad spend at or above the agreed minimum ($2,500/month on Revenue Engine, $5,000/month on Scale) for the full 90 days, paid directly to the ad platforms.
  3. Share your past-customer list for the comeback campaign.
  4. Keep the AI receptionist, missed-call text-back and follow-up automations switched on.
  5. Contact booked leads within 1 business hour during opening hours, and log the outcome in [Brand] OS.
  6. Attend the monthly review call (or reschedule within the same week).
  7. Tell us before changing your prices, services, opening hours or offers.
  8. Keep your account in good standing (invoices paid within 7 days).

We track each of these in your dashboard, so you always know where you stand. If something slips, we'll tell you the same week.

If we miss

  • Live-in-14: your first monthly fee is billed on the day the receptionist and text-back go live, not before. This happens automatically. You don't need to ask.
  • Double-or-Work-Free: within 7 days of the end of the guarantee period, we send you a revenue report. If the target wasn't met and your part was done, your monthly fee pauses and we keep working for up to 90 more days. Billing restarts the month after you hit the target, or at the end of the 90 extra days, whichever comes first.
  • You can also raise it yourself: email [contact email] with "Guarantee review" in the subject line. We reply within 2 business days.

What's not covered

  • Setup fees are non-refundable. They pay for the work done in your first weeks: your website, CRM build, automations and AI receptionist training.
  • Ad spend goes directly to Meta and Google and isn't refundable by us.
  • Usage charges above your plan's allowance (calls, texts, AI minutes) are billed as normal.
  • The guarantee isn't a cash refund. It's continued work at no monthly fee.
  • Foundation and à la carte services aren't covered by the Double-or-Work-Free Guarantee. Foundation includes the Live-in-14 Promise.

Questions

Why don't you just offer a money-back guarantee?

Most of our work happens in your first weeks: building your system and running your ads. A refund wouldn't get you what you came for. Continuing to work until you hit the target does.

What if my revenue drops for reasons outside both our control?

Things like a natural disaster, losing a licence, or a key staff member leaving can pause the guarantee period. We'll agree a new end date in writing.

Do you need access to my bank account?

No. Read-only reports or statements for the baseline months and the guarantee period are enough. You choose how to share them.
