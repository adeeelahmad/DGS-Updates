# Cold Email Sequence: Double Revenue in 90 Days

Four emails over 12 days to US local service business owners. Every email sells the **outcome** (double revenue in 90 days), backs it with the **whole done-for-you system** and the **guarantee**, and uses one research finding as **proof**. The offer is a free 90-Day Double-Revenue Plan. The goal of every email is a reply, not a click.

## The message every email is built on

**Headline:** We double local service businesses' revenue in 90 days, or we keep working free until we do.

**The five things we do, all done for you:**

1. **More new customers:** Meta and Google ads, and postcards to the neighborhoods they serve
2. **Every lead answered:** an AI receptionist that picks up 24/7
3. **Every lead booked:** follow-up that texts, emails and books until they show up
4. **Past customers back:** comeback campaigns by email, postcard and voicemail
5. **Chosen over competitors:** more 5-star reviews and a website that books

**Proof line:** one true thing we found about their business. It shows there's room to grow. It's never the topic of the email.

**Ask:** "Want me to send your free 90-day plan?"

## Before you send

- **Sending setup:** separate domains (never your main one), 2–3 inboxes each, SPF/DKIM/DMARC set, warmed for 14–21 days, 30–40 sends per inbox per day.
- **List:** verified emails only. One trade per campaign, so the copy can name it.
- **Format:** plain text, under 120 words, no images, no links in email 1, no attachments.
- **Stop rules:** any reply (positive, negative or "remove me") stops the sequence for that contact. Honor unsubscribes immediately.
- **Claims:** only promise what your guarantee terms page and contract say. Only quote results you can prove.
- **Footer (CAN-SPAM), on every email:**

  ```
  [Brand] · [physical mailing address]
  Not relevant? Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.
  ```

## After the sequence

- **No reply:** move to nurture. Re-contact in 3–6 months with a new proof line. Circumstances change.
- **Calls:** the setter calls everyone who opened 2+ times: "I sent you a couple of emails about doubling {{Company}}'s revenue and wanted to follow up."
- **Mail:** for your best-fit prospects, mail a printed one-page plan preview between email 1 and the first call.
- **LinkedIn:** connection request on day 1, short DM on day 5 if they accepted.

## Weekly numbers to track

| Metric | Target to start | Action if below |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate | 40%+ | Check deliverability first, then test subject lines |
| Reply rate | 2–5% | Test proof lines and the email 1 wording |
| Positive reply rate | 0.5–1.5% | Test the ask (e.g. "free plan" vs "free 14-day trial of the system") |
| Positive replies that book | 50%+ | Reply faster: within 5 minutes during US work hours |

Change **one thing per week**, log the result, and keep the winner.
